How an answer is built

A retrieval-augmented pipeline: the model may only speak from what was retrieved, and says so when nothing fits.

  1. 1

    Your question is understood in its language

    English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yorùbá or Igbo, SMS spellings and all ("hw much is d pasport"), including follow-ups like "e free?" that depend on the previous turn. Greetings and thanks get a quick reply in your language.

  2. 2

    Verified entries are retrieved

    A knowledge base built from NIMC, NIS, FRSC and NPC publications, each entry dated and linked to its official source, searched by meaning and by keyword together.

  3. 3

    The reply cites what it used

    Figures and steps come only from those entries, with numbered sources you can open. When you need to act, the official channel is named. If nothing matches, it tells you instead of guessing.

One question, five languages

English, Nigerian Pidgin and Hausa are written natively across the whole knowledge base. Yorùbá and Igbo are answered from the same verified facts, and native entries are growing.

Information and guidance, not a government portal

Tambaya NG cannot enrol you, issue documents, take payments or look up your records, it never asks for your NIN or passport number, and it will not help with fake documents or skipping capture and tests. When something needs doing, it points you to the official NIMC, Immigration, FRSC or Population Commission channel.

Tambaya NG is a seminar project of the Department of Computer Science, Yaba College of Technology. Information is drawn from official agency publications and dated at each entry; always confirm fees on the official portal at the time of payment.
